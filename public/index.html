          How to play Runo

          Be the first to empty your hand. 2–4 players, 7 cards each. The first discard is always a number card, and a random player starts.

          On your turn, do one thing

          • Play a card that matches the active color or the top card's number or symbol. Wild and Wild +4 can always be played, even if you have the color.
          • Draw one card. Drawing always ends your turn, even if the new card would fit.

          Action cards

          • Skip: the next player misses a turn.
          • Reverse: play changes direction. With 2 players it works like a Skip.
          • +2 / Wild +4: the next player draws 2 or 4 and misses a turn. No stacking, no challenges.
          • Wild: you choose the new color.
          • With 2 players, Skip, Reverse, +2 and +4 all give you another turn.

          Calling RUNO!

          • When you're down to one card, on your turn, press RUNO! before you play it.
          • You can't call it early (with two cards) or on someone else's turn. Calling doesn't end your turn.
          • Forget, and your last card bounces back: you keep it, draw 2, and your turn ends.
          • Drawing any card clears your call.

          Winning

          Empty your hand to win the round. A last action card still counts, so a final +2 still makes the next player draw. Each win adds to your tally for as long as the room lasts.
